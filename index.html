01Transfer protocol▾

Your practice swing already works. The ball takes it away. These six rules govern every block.

  1. Rehearsal and shot are one unit. Rehearse, step in, hit within five seconds.
  2. Same speed both times. A slower rehearsal is not a rehearsal.
  3. Look 10 cm ahead of the ball, or at its front edge. Never the back.
  4. Grip pressure 4 out of 10 — check it is still 4 at the top.
  5. Cut your time over the ball. Deliberation is tension, and tension is how the ball wins.
  6. Score the swing, not the shot. Rate 1–5 on how close it felt to the rehearsal. Ignore where it went.

APosture & ball transfer▾

First block every session, never cut. Face spray every fifth shot — strike location is the scoreboard, not ball flight.

Pick three, not nine

One of them is always the chair drill. If the rehearsal divot test came back clean, weight toward the transfer four.

BChipping low point▾

Narrow stance, feet slightly open, ball just back of centre, handle 2–4 cm ahead, 70% pressure lead foot — and it stays there.

Graduation

At 16 of 20 on the line drill, add the landing ladder — towels at 3, 6 and 9 m, five balls each, random order.

CLow point confirmation▾

The goal, restated

Not a divot. A mark after the ball, however shallow. A bruise or a scuff counts. If the club leaves nothing, low point is still at or behind the ball. If it scuffs — you are done.

Grass only

On a mat the club skids, so contact 2 cm behind the ball still flies. Mats cannot teach low point. Every drill in this block happens on grass.

Desensitisation ladder. Tap your current rung. Move up only when the current one is boring — two or three sessions each.

Throughout: grip pressure 4/10 — tight grips send shock into the wrists, soft ones absorb it. Discomfort is expected; pain is not. Stop and get it looked at.

Drills, once the ladder is done

Before blaming your swing for spin

Check the stamped loft. A 23–24° hollow-body "5-iron" at 75 mph will not stop quickly regardless of delivery. Cheaper levers: clean grooves, dry face and ball, centred strikes.

DDriver▾

Last block, always. Never in the same 20 minutes as iron low point work.

Setup checklist

  • Ball off the lead heel
  • Half the ball above the crown
  • Stance wider than your irons
  • Trail shoulder lower, spine tilted away 5–10°
  • 55% pressure trail foot, hands level with or behind the ball
  • No forward press

04On the course▾

For now

Take the divot question off the table entirely. Play the strike you have — your iron results are already good on average, and a shallow delivery is a legitimate way to play golf, not a fault to fix mid-round. The ladder belongs on the range. Manufacturing a divot on the course is exactly how you produce the duffs that started the flinch.
